Double Dash grew from a rare add-on to nearly half of March’s orders

Double Dash accounted for 19 of 86 deliveries between July 2025 and July 2026 — 22% of the total. It barely registered at first: none at all in the opening two months, and never more than one a month through the end of 2025. That changed in the new year, and the habit peaked in March 2026, when 7 of that month’s 16 orders were Double Dash.

Note: one segment counts one delivery — a Double Dash pairs two stores into a single drop-off, so it is counted once, not twice. Source: DoorDash order receipts, July 2025 – July 2026.
